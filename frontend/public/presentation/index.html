The problem

You carry five cards.
You use the wrong one.

Every card has a different multiplier, point value, and network. Nobody does that math at the register, so the debit card comes out and the rewards stay on the table.

Too much math

4x points at 1.8¢ vs 2x at 1.4¢ vs 5% on one category. In two seconds, at checkout.

Hidden rules

Costco only takes Visa. Target is coded as a discount store, so grocery bonuses don't apply.

Coupons live elsewhere

The best promo code and the best card are never shown in the same place.

The solution

One engine. Every way you shop.
And it plays like a game.

Wallet + Pay

Every card shows what it earns at the store you're in. Hold to pay; coins rain.

Scan

Screenshot an online cart. Gemini reads it; we stack coupon + card.

Deals

Rewards

Your capture score, best-card streak, and what the wrong card cost you.

How it works

One pure rewards engine,
four screens.

React PWA

Vite + TypeScript, Tailwind, Motion springs, confetti and coin showers, Web Audio chime and fanfare, haptics. Installs to your home screen.

→

FastAPI + Engine

Network filter → MCC category → yield ranking. Triple Stack and missed-cashback math as pure, tested functions.

→

Gemini

Cart screenshot parsing, simulated coupons for unknown stores.

Tiger Data

Postgres + TimescaleDB hypertable of every purchase.

yield % = multiplier × ¢ per point  ·  missed = best card value − card used value
